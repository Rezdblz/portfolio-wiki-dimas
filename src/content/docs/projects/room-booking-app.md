## Overview

A room booking application: users reserve rooms, and the backend enforces who is allowed to book and when.

## My role

Backend Developer, February 2026 — API design, data model, and validation.

## What I built

- RESTful APIs in C#, ASP.NET Core, and PostgreSQL.
- CRUD operations for rooms and bookings.
- Role-based authentication, so booking rights follow the user's role.
- Database-level validation to keep conflicting or malformed bookings out.

## Stack

C# · ASP.NET Core · PostgreSQL
